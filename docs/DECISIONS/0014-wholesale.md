# ADR 0014 — Wholesale accounts, price lists, MOQ and quotations

- **Status:** accepted (M6, 2026-11-16)
- **Context:** the site only sells at retail prices to guests and (since M5) signed-in shoppers; business buyers (distributors, retailers, HoReCa) need approved accounts, their own prices, minimum order quantities, quotes for large baskets and payment terms. The roadmap left open: tiers vs per-account pricing, MOQ values, credit terms, approval criteria, distributor territories.
- **Decisions**
  - **A buyer is a signed-in customer with an APPROVED `BusinessAccount`.** Identity reuses M5 (emailed code), so a business account inherits a verified email; customer sign-in is off in production until M7, so wholesale self-service is dormant there too. Staff can pre-create an account from a business enquiry with only a contact email: the verified owner of that address is linked on their first sign-in (a customer holds at most one account).
  - **Application and approval.** `POST /api/business/apply` (GSTIN format and mod-36 check character validated; one live account per GSTIN by partial unique index; a rejected applicant can correct and re-apply). Staff move accounts through `pending -> approved | rejected | closed`, `approved <-> suspended`, `suspended -> closed`, `rejected -> approved`; `closed` is terminal. Approval criteria are the staff's call (the system records the decision and a private note). Territories are not modelled.
  - **Pricing: per-account price list with quantity tiers.** `PriceList` + `PriceListItem(variant, minQty, unitPrice)`; an account points at one list. Resolution is account -> its ACTIVE list -> the tier with the highest `minQty` the quantity reaches -> otherwise the **retail selling price** (the roadmap's "MRP fallback" is read as "no better than retail": falling back to the higher MRP would make wholesale dearer than the shop). Staff-side rules: distinct `minQty`, a larger quantity never costs more per unit, no tier above the variant's MRP, positive integer paise (DB CHECKs back this up).
  - **MOQ and case packs** (`VariantWholesale`, absent = unrestricted) apply to wholesale carts, orders and quote requests only; retail checkout is untouched (tests prove a retail order below a variant's wholesale MOQ still works, and the retail 50-unit line cap is unchanged). Wholesale lines may go up to 100,000 units.
  - **Separate order path, no leakage.** Wholesale orders go through `POST /api/business/orders` (and quote acceptance), which call `createOrder` with a `WholesaleHooks` object (price lines, delivery fee, credit check, post-create claim). `/api/orders`, `/api/cart/validate`, `/api/products*` and the catalogue never import wholesale code (ratchet test) and are byte-for-byte identical for a signed-in buyer and an anonymous visitor (test). `/api/business/*` is gated by `requireBusiness` (401 no session, 403 `business_account_required` / `business_account_not_active`); the account, price list and quote ids always come from the session, never the request.
  - **Payment terms.** `prepaid` (default) or `credit` with a `creditLimit` (paise). Wholesale orders are always **offline-payment** orders (a request-only attempt, `paymentMethod "cod"`, staff confirm payment with the existing audited flow); there is no online checkout for B2B in M6. Credit is enforced when an order is created or a quote accepted: outstanding = the account's non-cancelled, not-yet-paid orders; new total must fit under the limit, checked under a row lock so parallel orders cannot jointly overrun it. Prepaid accounts have no system check: collecting payment before dispatch is operational (M7/M10 bring invoices and collection).
  - **Quotes (`requested -> quoted -> accepted`)** run through `transitionQuote`, the only mover (conditional update + `QuoteEvent`, actor rules: staff price/reject, the buyer accepts/declines, the system expires; terminal states stay terminal; re-quoting a `quoted` quote revises it). The buyer sees no price until staff quote. Validity is 1-30 days (default 7). **Accepting is one transaction:** the order at exactly the quoted prices and delivery fee (stock held, credit checked, total verified equal to the quote's) and the quote's move to `accepted`; any failure (stock, credit, expiry, a concurrent accept) rolls back everything and the quote stays `quoted`. A quote past its validity is refused and marked expired; the scheduled job (`/api/internal/orders/expire`, now also reporting `quotesExpired`) sweeps the rest. Closing an account cancels its open quotes in the same transaction.
  - **Admin API:** accounts (list/get/patch), enquiry conversion, price lists and tiers, MOQ, quotes. New permissions: `wholesale:read` (owner, operations, catalog), `wholesale:write` (owner, operations), `pricing:write` (owner, catalog). Every mutation is audited with ids and field names only (no prices, notes or reasons); the route ratchet enforces `requireAdmin` + `writeAudit`. All admin money fields are integer **paise**.
  - **Erasure interplay (M5):** a live (pending/approved/suspended) business account blocks customer erasure (`409 business_account`; staff close it first). After closing, erasure anonymises the contact person (name, email, phone, notes) but keeps the company name and GSTIN (a company's tax identity). The data export includes the business account and its quotes.
- **Not done / for the business to confirm**
  - **B2B GST invoices, e-way bills and credit invoicing are M7**; prepaid-before-dispatch is not system-enforced.
  - **No customer or admin UI** (M8). Distributor territories, per-account tier overrides, multi-user accounts (several staff per company) and quote versions/PDFs are not modelled. The GSTIN is checked for form and check character only, not looked up on the GST portal.
  - The decisions above are conservative defaults: MOQs, tiers, credit limits and approval rules are data the business sets, not code.
